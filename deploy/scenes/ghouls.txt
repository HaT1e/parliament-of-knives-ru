You take a moment to procure a burner phone and make a call to the number Gerard gave your new driver, Sam. He answers it quickly, but in a less than professional manner. 

"Um, hello?" you hear the newly bonded ghoul's voice on the other end of the line. "This is Sam," he adds belatedly.

"Sam," you say. "I need a ride as soon as possible. Has Gerard purchased you a vehicle to my specifications?"

"Yes, ${sirmaam}. I'll be right there." He hangs up.

You stare at the phone with increasing irritation. You knew that Sam would require a little work to break in, but there's only so much ineptitude you can tolerate. The phone rings again and you take the call.

"Um, boss?"

"Yes?"

He clears his throat. "I forgot to ask where you were."

Sam arrives in a dark blue Lexus ES. It's sleek, but a bit small for your liking—not exactly the kind of car that you're accustomed to being chauffeured around in. Then again, you'd asked Gerard for lower-profile than your usual ride, and he'd delivered. You sigh. At least the windows are tinted.
